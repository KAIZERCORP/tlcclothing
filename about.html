ABOUT TLC

The Story Behind

From a campus dream to Mukono's most trusted fashion label.

Founded by Tom Lowkii Choudry

TLC — "The Lord Cares" — was born from a vision to create quality apparel with a message.

What started as a campus hustle at Uganda Christian University has grown into a trusted fashion name across Uganda, Kenya, and even Dubai.

With a 4.9★ rating and 63 glowing reviews, TLC is known for premium T‑shirts, streetwear, and bold designs that speak to the modern African spirit.
