ABOUT TLC

The Story Behind

From a campus dream to a fashion label built around quality, identity and purpose.

Founded by Tom Lowkii Choudry

TLC — “The Lord Cares” — was born from a vision to create quality apparel with a message.

What started as a campus hustle at Uganda Christian University has grown into a fashion name serving customers in Uganda and beyond.

Our focus is simple: premium-feeling pieces, confident design and a brand people can identify with.
