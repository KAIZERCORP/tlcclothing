The Story Behind TLC

From a campus dream to a regional fashion force.

📍 Bishop Tucker Rd

Founded by Tom Lowkii Choudry

TLC — "The Lord Cares" — was born from a vision to create quality apparel with a message. What started as a campus hustle at Uganda Christian University has grown into a trusted fashion name across Uganda, Kenya, and even Dubai.

With a 4.9★ rating and 63 glowing reviews, TLC is known for premium T‑shirts, streetwear, and bold designs that speak to the modern African spirit.

Our Mission: To dress the confident African with style that tells a story.
Bishop Tucker Road, Mukono — opposite the Digital Hub.
